Frequently Asked Questions

What formats are supported?

PDF to Anything supports 25+ output formats including DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPEG, EPUB, HTML, JSON, XML, and many more.

Can I convert multiple PDFs?

Yes! You can upload and convert up to 50 PDF files at once.

How large can my PDF be?

Maximum file size is 500 MB per file and 5000 pages per PDF.

How long are files stored?

Files are automatically deleted after 2 hours. You can download results immediately after conversion.

Do I need an account?

No! PDF to Anything is completely anonymous. No registration or login required.

Is it secure?

Yes. Files are processed securely and deleted automatically. We do not retain files after the retention period.
